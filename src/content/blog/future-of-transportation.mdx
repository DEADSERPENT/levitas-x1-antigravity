Seoul's experimental floating bus line launched last month. Key advantages:

1. **No infrastructure damage** - Buses don't touch the road
2. **Zero vibration** - Comfortable for passengers
3. **Route flexibility** - Can navigate around obstacles
4. **Energy efficiency** - Rolling resistance eliminated
